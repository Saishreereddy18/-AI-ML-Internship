# Responsible AI Case Study Report

## 1. Introduction

Responsible AI refers to developing and using Artificial Intelligence systems in a fair, transparent, secure, and accountable manner.

This case study examines racial bias in a healthcare AI algorithm.

## 2. Case Study

**Title:** Racial Bias in a Healthcare Prediction Algorithm

**Research Paper:** Dissecting Racial Bias in an Algorithm Used to Manage the Health of Populations

**Authors:** Ziad Obermeyer, Brian Powers, Christine Vogeli, and Sendhil Mullainathan.

**Published:** Science Journal, October 2019.

## 3. What Happened?

A healthcare prediction algorithm was used by hospitals to identify patients who needed additional medical care.

The algorithm predicted future healthcare costs instead of directly predicting patients' actual health needs.

Due to differences in healthcare access, Black patients often had lower healthcare costs than White patients with similar medical conditions.

As a result, the algorithm incorrectly identified many Black patients as having lower healthcare needs.

## 4. Impact

- Black patients were less likely to receive additional medical support.
- Healthcare resources were not distributed fairly.
- The algorithm created racial disparities in healthcare decisions.
- Many patients who needed medical attention were overlooked.

The researchers estimated that correcting the bias would increase the percentage of Black patients receiving additional help from 17.7% to 46.5%.

## 5. Main Ethical Issues

1. **Bias:** The algorithm produced unfair outcomes for Black patients.
2. **Fairness:** Healthcare resources were not allocated equally.
3. **Transparency:** The use of healthcare costs as a prediction target was problematic.
4. **Accountability:** AI decisions required proper evaluation and human oversight.

## 6. How Could This Problem Have Been Prevented?

- Use representative and diverse training datasets.
- Select appropriate prediction targets.
- Regularly evaluate model performance across different groups.
- Conduct fairness and bias audits.
- Include human experts in important healthcare decisions.
- Monitor AI systems after deployment.

## 7. Conclusion

This case study demonstrates that AI systems can produce unfair results even when their predictions appear accurate.

Responsible AI requires fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, and continuous monitoring.

AI should support human decision-making rather than replace human judgment.
