# Responsible AI – Prevention Recommendations

## 1. Introduction

Responsible AI ensures that Artificial Intelligence systems are developed and deployed safely, fairly, and transparently.

Based on the healthcare AI bias case study, the following recommendations can help prevent ethical issues in AI systems.

## 2. Bias Prevention

- Use diverse and representative datasets.
- Check datasets for missing values and imbalance.
- Evaluate model performance across different demographic groups.
- Conduct regular bias audits.
- Avoid using biased features or inappropriate prediction targets.

## 3. Privacy Protection

- Collect only necessary user information.
- Remove or anonymize personally identifiable information.
- Use encryption to protect sensitive data.
- Restrict access to authorized users.
- Follow applicable data protection regulations.

## 4. Hallucination Prevention

- Use reliable and verified data sources.
- Apply Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) where appropriate.
- Provide references or sources for AI-generated answers.
- Set confidence thresholds for uncertain outputs.
- Allow users to verify important information.

## 5. Transparency

- Explain how AI systems make important decisions.
- Inform users when they are interacting with AI.
- Clearly communicate system limitations.
- Maintain proper documentation of datasets and models.

## 6. Human Oversight

- Include human review in high-impact decisions.
- Allow users to question or appeal AI decisions.
- Do not depend entirely on AI predictions.
- Assign responsibility for monitoring AI systems.

## 7. Continuous Monitoring

- Regularly test AI models after deployment.
- Monitor accuracy, fairness, and reliability.
- Identify unexpected outputs and system failures.
- Update models and datasets when necessary.

## 8. Conclusion

Preventing AI ethical issues requires continuous evaluation, responsible data handling, transparency, and human supervision.

Following these recommendations helps build trustworthy and reliable AI systems.
