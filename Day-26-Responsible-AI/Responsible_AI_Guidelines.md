# Responsible AI Guidelines for Our Company

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines define how our company should develop, test, deploy, and maintain AI systems responsibly.

Our goal is to ensure that AI applications are fair, secure, transparent, reliable, and beneficial to users.

## 2. Fairness and Bias Prevention

- Use diverse and representative training datasets.
- Check datasets for demographic bias.
- Test AI models across different user groups.
- Avoid discriminatory predictions and decisions.
- Perform regular fairness audits.

## 3. Data Privacy and Security

- Collect only the information required for the application.
- Never expose confidential or personal information.
- Anonymize sensitive data wherever possible.
- Use secure data storage and encryption.
- Allow access only to authorized employees.

## 4. Transparency and Explainability

- Inform users when they interact with AI systems.
- Explain important AI-generated decisions.
- Document model limitations and capabilities.
- Provide sources when AI systems generate factual answers.

## 5. Hallucination and Accuracy Control

- Use reliable and verified information sources.
- Validate AI-generated responses.
- Use RAG when appropriate to ground answers in trusted documents.
- Clearly communicate uncertainty.
- Do not present unverified AI outputs as facts.

## 6. Human Oversight

- Require human review for high-impact decisions.
- Do not allow AI to independently make critical decisions without appropriate supervision.
- Provide a process for users to report incorrect AI decisions.
- Assign responsible team members to review AI outputs.

## 7. Responsible AI Development

- Follow ethical AI development practices.
- Test applications before deployment.
- Maintain documentation of models and datasets.
- Evaluate performance, fairness, and security regularly.

## 8. Monitoring and Accountability

- Monitor deployed AI systems continuously.
- Maintain records of important AI decisions.
- Investigate reported issues.
- Correct harmful or inaccurate outputs.
- Update systems whenever necessary.

## 9. Employee Responsibilities

Every employee working with AI must:

- Follow company data privacy policies.
- Avoid using biased or unauthorized datasets.
- Verify important AI-generated information.
- Report ethical concerns and system failures.
- Use AI tools responsibly.

## 10. Conclusion

Our company is committed to developing AI systems that respect human values, protect user privacy, prevent discrimination, and maintain transparency.

Responsible AI is a continuous responsibility throughout the AI development lifecycle.
